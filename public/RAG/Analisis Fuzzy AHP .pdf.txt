3. Rumus Dasar AHP
Normalisasi matriks
[
n_{ij} = \frac{a_{ij}}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{ij}}
]

Bobot prioritas
[
w_i = \frac{\sum_{j=1}^{n} n_{ij}}{n}

Lambda maksimum
[
\lambda_{max} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{(Aw)_i}{w_i}
]
Consistency Index
[
CI = \frac{\lambda_{max}-n}{n-1}
]
Consistency RaJo
[
CR = \frac{CI}{RI}

Matriks dinyatakan konsisten jika:
